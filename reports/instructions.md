# How to Fill Out the Weekly Status Report

Copy `template.md`, rename it (for example `status-2026-10-25.md`) and replace every `<placeholder>`. Delete any section that doesn't apply, but keep the headings in the same order so reports are easy to compare.

## General rules
- Write for a reader who has two minutes. Lead with outcomes, not activity.
- Keep each bullet to one line where possible.
- Use dates in `YYYY-MM-DD` format and name a single owner for every action.
- Be honest about status. A reported Amber early is more useful than a surprise Red later.

## Header
- **Project:** the project or team name.
- **Week of:** the Monday and Friday of the reporting week.
- **Prepared by:** who is responsible for the report's accuracy.
- **Overall status:**
  - **Green:** on track, with no help needed.
  - **Amber:** at risk. A problem exists, but there is a plan to recover.
  - **Red:** off track. Scope, date or budget will be missed without intervention.

## Summary
Two or three sentences. State the overall result, the main risk and any decision needed. Write it last, after the other sections.

## Accomplishments
List completed work, not work in progress. Describe the result and its value, for example "Released login feature to staging" rather than "Worked on login". Aim for 3 to 6 bullets.

## Metrics
Add the numbers your stakeholders care about, such as stories completed, defects, or budget burn. Always fill in last week's value and the target so the trend is visible. Keep the table to five rows or fewer.

## Blockers and Risks
Include only items that threaten scope, date or budget.
- **Impact:** say what happens if the item is not resolved.
- **Owner:** one named person, not a team.
- **Needed by:** the date after which the impact gets worse.
- **Action or help needed:** the specific next step or request.

If there are none, write "None this week."

## Decisions Needed
List decisions you need from someone else. Say who decides and by when. Leave this out if there are none.

## Next Week's Plan
List up to five priorities in order of importance. Each should be specific enough that you can say next week whether it was done.

## Before you send
- Does the overall status match the blockers listed?
- Are all placeholders replaced?
- Does every blocker have an owner and a date?
- Does the summary agree with the rest of the report?
